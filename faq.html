Questions & answers

Data reporting, explained in plain English.

The questions we get asked most about Power BI, Excel, automation and how we work — answered simply, with no jargon.

How much does data reporting cost?

It depends on the size of the job, and we always agree a price before starting. A one-off report or spreadsheet clean-up is usually a fixed price; ongoing upkeep and automation works well on a monthly retainer. Whatever the shape, you get a clear quote up front — no open-ended bills, no surprises. The first conversation and data review are free.

Power BI or Excel — which do I need?

Excel is perfect for detailed, hands-on models and one-off analysis you want full control over. Power BI shines when you need live, interactive dashboards that refresh on their own and are shared across a team. Plenty of businesses use both, and part of our job is recommending the right tool for what you're actually trying to do — rather than forcing everything into one.

What exactly is "report automation"?

It means setting your reports up so they refresh and deliver themselves on a schedule, instead of someone rebuilding the same thing by hand every week or month. The benefits are simple: hours of time given back, no more copy-paste mistakes, and numbers that are always ready when a decision needs making. If you've got a report that quietly eats a morning every week, that's exactly the kind of thing automation fixes.

What is a bank statement income report?

It turns raw, messy bank statements into a clear, categorised picture of income and outgoings. It's commonly used for affordability and lending decisions, or simply when someone needs a trustworthy view of where money is genuinely coming from and going — without wading through hundreds of transactions by hand.

Can you find errors and discrepancies in our data?

Yes — it's one of the things we're asked for most. When two systems disagree, or a report never quite ties out, we cross-check the numbers across sources, track down where the mismatch comes from, and explain why it happened, not just that it did. The same approach surfaces quiet losses: leakage, duplicate costs and missed revenue that add up over time.

Do you only work with North West businesses?

We're based in Merseyside and love working with businesses across the North West, but we work with clients right across the UK remotely too. Data work is done securely online, so where you're based is rarely a barrier — you get the same service whether you're down the road or at the other end of the country.

Is our data kept confidential?

Always. Your data is treated as strictly confidential, used only to deliver the work we've agreed, and handled in line with UK GDPR. It's never shared with third parties without your say-so, and it's returned or securely deleted whenever you ask.

Got a question that isn't here?

Ask us directly — tell us what you're trying to sort with your data and we'll point you at the shortest way to fix it. Free, and no pressure.
