When the numbers don't agree, find out why.

This is the forensic end of the work: two systems disagreeing, a report that never quite ties, or money going somewhere nobody can explain.

Cross-examination

We put your numbers side by side across systems, periods and records to confirm they actually agree — and hold up when someone challenges them.

  • Record-level, not just totals
  • A repeatable check, not a one-off

Finding discrepancies

Track down the mismatches between systems and reports — and explain exactly why they happened, not just that they did.

  • Root cause, in plain English
  • A fix so it stops recurring
