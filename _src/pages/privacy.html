Privacy

Privacy notice.

This notice covers this website and how enquiries sent through it are handled. Last updated: 8 September 2026.

Who we are

Data Insights North West is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. You can contact us at enquiries@datainsightsnorthwest.co.uk. We are a service-area business and do not publish a postal address.

What we collect

The only personal data we collect through this website is what you choose to type into the contact form: your name, your email address, your company name if you give one, the service you selected, and your message.

How the contact form works

The form is submitted to Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards your enquiry to our email inbox. Our business email is provided by Cloudflare Email Routing, which forwards messages to a Microsoft Outlook mailbox.

Why we use your data, and our lawful basis

We use the information you provide to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the agreed work. Our lawful basis for replying to an enquiry is legitimate interests — responding to people who have asked us a question. Where work is agreed, our lawful basis is contract.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind. That is also why there is no cookie banner — there is nothing on this site that requires consent. If this ever changes, this notice will be updated first.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Both providers process technical connection data, such as your IP address, in their own server logs for security and delivery purposes.

Your data during a project

If you become a client, any data you share with us for the work is treated as strictly confidential. It is used only for the agreed work, is not shared with third parties without your consent, and is returned or securely deleted on request or at the end of the engagement.

How long we keep data

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and any resulting work, plus any period we are required to keep records for tax and accounting purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected, have it erased, restrict or object to how it's used, and ask for it in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, email enquiries@datainsightsnorthwest.co.uk.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are unhappy with how your data has been handled.

What we don't do

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling, we do not send marketing emails, and we do not sell your data.
