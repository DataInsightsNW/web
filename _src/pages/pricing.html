Pricing

A price agreed before anything starts.

Two ways to work: a fixed price for a one-off piece of work, or a monthly retainer for reporting that needs keeping alive. No hourly meter running in the background, and no open-ended bills.

One-off work — fixed price

A report to build, a workbook to rebuild, a discrepancy to chase down, an analysis to run. We scope it, quote a single fixed price, and that's the price.

  • Quoted in writing before work starts
  • Agreed deliverable, so "done" is not a debate
  • If the scope changes, we requote before continuing

Ongoing upkeep — monthly retainer

Once reports are live they need refresh checks, small changes and the occasional new question answered. A retainer covers that for a set number of hours a month.

  • Rolling monthly, no minimum term
  • Cancel any time with 30 days' notice
  • Fees reviewed no more than once a year, with 30 days' notice

Pricing questions

Why isn't there an hourly rate on the site?

Because an hourly rate tells you almost nothing useful. Two analysts quoting the same rate can take wildly different amounts of time on the same job, and an hourly price puts all the risk of that on you. A fixed price for an agreed deliverable moves that risk to us, which is where it belongs.

There is an hourly figure — £80 — but it only applies to extra work beyond a retainer's monthly hours, and it's always agreed with you before it's incurred.

Do I have to take a retainer?

No. Plenty of work is a single fixed-price job with no ongoing commitment. A retainer only makes sense once you have live reporting that genuinely needs maintaining — if it doesn't, we'll say so.

What if the job turns out to be bigger than expected?

We come back to you with a revised quote before doing the extra work, not after. You then decide whether to go ahead, trim the scope, or stop. You will never receive an invoice for work you didn't agree to.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes, including the initial data review. You get the findings — where the time is going and what we'd tackle first — whether or not you go on to hire us. If it turns out you don't need us, that's a perfectly good outcome.

Can you work to a fixed budget?

Usually. Tell us the budget and we'll tell you honestly what can be done well within it, and what would have to wait. We'd rather scope to your number than deliver a compromised version of something bigger.
